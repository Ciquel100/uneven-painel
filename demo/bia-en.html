What to try

  1. Ask for a time: "do you have anything Friday afternoon?" — she replies with real open slots, computed from the calendar.
  2. Ask a price: "how much is a haircut?" — she knows the services because they were entered, not invented.
  3. Book: give the service, the day, the time and your first name. She only confirms after the calendar accepted it.
  4. Ask for a person: "I want to talk to someone" — she says a person will take over and goes quiet. In a real business, the owner's phone buzzes at that moment.
  5. Switch language mid-conversation — she follows.
